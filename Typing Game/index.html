🖐️ Finger School
Professor Quill's lesson: how to type with two hands and never look down.

1️⃣ Sit like a wizard

  • Both feet flat on the floor, back tall against the chair.
  • Elbows bent, wrists floating — don't rest them on the desk.
  • Screen straight ahead. Your eyes stay on the screen, not on your hands.
  • Curl your fingers a little, like you're holding a small apple. 🍎

2️⃣ The Home Row — where your fingers sleep 🛏️

Eight fingers each get their own home key in the middle row. Every time you finish a letter, your finger hops straight back home.

4️⃣ Try it now: find the two secret bumps 🔎

Look away from the keyboard. Slide your fingers along the middle row until you feel two tiny bumps. Put your left pointer on F and your right pointer on J — those bumps are how your hands find home in the dark. Press them both now!

5️⃣ Two hands, sharing the work

  • Capital letters: hold Shift with the pinky of the opposite hand. Capital G (left hand) → right pinky holds Shift.
  • Space bar: always a thumb — usually the thumb of whichever hand just finished.
  • Backspace: right pinky. It's the undo charm. 🪄
  • Slow and right beats fast and wrong. Speed arrives on its own once your fingers know the way home.
