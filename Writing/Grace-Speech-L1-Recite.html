Public Speaking · Lesson 1 · Speech to learn by heart

The Quiet Girl

By Grace — built from your own stories in Steps 1 and 2. About 2 minutes out loud.

How to learn it today

  1. Read the whole speech out loud twice in mode 1.
  2. Learn it one coloured box at a time. Say the box, look away, say it again.
  3. Switch to mode 2. Say every word from just the first letters.
  4. Switch to mode 3. Say the whole speech from the cues only.
  5. Stand up, and say it to Mum or Dad with no screen at all.

‖ means stop for one breath. Bold words are said louder.

You don't have to say every word perfectly. If you know the cues, you know the speech.
