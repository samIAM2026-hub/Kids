How this works

Ten lessons — the whole of Level 1. Each one starts with the lesson itself: five minutes of reading, with a worked speech to look at. Then you build a speech of your own in five steps.

Step 1 learn the idea · Step 2 write three openings · Step 3 build the skeleton · Step 4 write the ending · Step 5 put it together, then read it out loud and record it.

Steps are an order, not a timetable. One sitting, one step is the usual shape — but take two when it is going well, and leave a big one half finished when it is not. Nothing here expires and nothing is late.

Each step you hand in, Dad or Mom reads and writes back. Nobody marks your spelling here — they are looking at whether it would hold a room.
