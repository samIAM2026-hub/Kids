The contest

Leaders of Tomorrow is holding a Thanksgiving video contest. You film yourself giving a short speech.

Ideas to talk about: what you are most thankful for this year · how you celebrate Thanksgiving · how you want to give back to your community. Here you use all three — one for each point.

The judges look at three things: content & thinking · expression & creativity · structure & organization.

Age groups: 8 and under · 9–11 · 12 and up. The best videos may be shown on LOT Academy’s social media.

How to do it: read the lesson first. Then Step 1 find your stories · Step 2 fill in your speech plan · Step 3 write your first and last line · Step 4 write it, learn it, and say it out loud. Last of all, film the video with Mum or Dad.

Each step you hand in, Dad or Mom reads and writes back.
