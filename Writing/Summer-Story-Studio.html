Who reads this, and how

An admissions officer reads a few hundred of these in a season, most of them in under two minutes. They are not grading your English — that is what the SSAT is for. They are deciding one thing: can they picture you in one of their classrooms, and do you sound like a real person they'd want there.

Everything below is aimed at that. You have written one of these already, so this is not a form to fill in — read the tips, then write the whole thing yourself, straight through.
