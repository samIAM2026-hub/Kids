A Few Things About Me

By Grace. About 1 minute out loud. Every word is yours — this page only adds marks to help you say it.

The shape of your speech

Remember the 6 pictures and you remember the speech. Each hobby has the same pattern: what I do → how it feels → the best part.

What the marks mean

  • bold say it fuller and stronger (not shouting)
  • | tiny stop, one beat
  • || stop and take a breath
  • wavy stretch it slowly: “re-e-ally”
  • ↗ voice goes up
  • ↘ voice goes down, like a full stop

The first words of each part

If you get stuck, you only need the start. The rest follows.

  1. ❓ Have you ever wondered…
  2. 💃 I really love dancing…
  3. 🎹 I also enjoy playing the piano…
  4. 🏸 I also like badminton…
  5. ✋ Dancing, piano, and badminton…
  6. 🙏 Now you know…

Love → enjoy → like: the three hobbies go from biggest feeling to smallest.

How to learn it fast

  1. Look at the 6 pictures. Say them out loud: question, dance, piano, badminton, all three, thank you.
  2. Mode 1: read the whole speech out loud twice. Follow the marks. Tap ▶ Hear it if you want to listen first.
  3. Learn one coloured box at a time. Say it, look away, say it again.
  4. Mode 2: the bold words are hidden. Say the whole speech, then tap a gap to check.
  5. Mode 3: only first letters. Mode 4: only pictures and cues.
  6. Stand up and say it to Mum or Dad with no screen. Press ⏱ Time me — about 1 minute is just right.

On the stage

  • Stand still, feet flat. Hands relaxed by your sides.
  • Take one breath before the first word.
  • Look at three people: one on the left, one in the middle, one on the right.
  • Being nervous makes you fast. Go slower than feels normal.
  • If you forget, pause, smile, and say the next picture. Nobody knows the words but you!
You don't have to say every word perfectly. If you know the pictures, you know the speech.
