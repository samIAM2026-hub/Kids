A column that makes exactly 10 still gets carried. Write 0 in that column and carry the 1.

✅ You did it 1342 + 2358 = 3700.
The rule: 10 ones is one whole ten with nothing left over → write 0 and carry 1. Ten of anything works the same way: 10 tens = 1 hundred, write 0 and carry 1.
