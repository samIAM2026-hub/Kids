👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 96–99

✅ You did it How long? Jump from the start to the next o'clock. Count the hours. Then count the minutes. Add the jumps.
What time at the end? Count forward: hours first, then minutes.
What time at the start? Count back.
Passing 12: after 12 noon, a.m. → p.m. After 12 midnight, p.m. → a.m.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 97–100

✅ All 8 done An hour has 60 minutes, not 100. So don't just take the times away like normal numbers — use jumps.
For a time like 2.06, the minutes box gets 06.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: taking times away like normal numbers (an hour is 60 minutes, not 100), forgetting to swap a.m. and p.m. after 12, counting forward when you need to count back, and minutes going past 60 without moving the hour on.

🏆 4 · The test — Workbook pages 101–104

18 questions — all of Workbook Practice 3, pages 101–104 · 1 minute each (18 minutes in all) · pass mark 80% (15 right).

For a time, type the hour in the first box and the minutes in the second box, then tap a.m. or p.m. For 2.06, type 2 and 06. Then press Check. You get one try per question — if it's wrong, you'll see why straight away.

Questions 4–6 in your book say "Draw a time line". Here you just type the answer. You can draw the time line on paper to help.
