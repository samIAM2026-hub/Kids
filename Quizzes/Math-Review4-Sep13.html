The greatest number from a set of digits (question 3) is its own little trick: put the digits in order, biggest first. With 7, 0, 1, 6 and 4 that gives 76,410 — and the 0 has to go last, because a 0 at the front would make it a 4-digit number.

🍕 Station 4 · comparing and ordering fractions

Two shortcuts get you through questions 15 and 16 without any common denominators:

1
Same top number? Then the one with the bigger bottom is smaller — the same amount shared between more people. So 29 < 27 < 23.
2
Compare against a half or a whole. 97 is more than 1 because the top is bigger than the bottom, so it beats all of them.
smallest to largest: 29 , 27 , 23 , 97

⚠️ What people lose marks on

Trap 1 · "3 times" — find the smaller one by dividing A computer costs $1857 and that is 3 times the printer. The printer is the small one, so it is $1857 ÷ 3 = $619, not × 3.
Trap 2 · reading −12 as bigger than −10 Further left on the number line is always smaller, so −12 < −10 even though 12 > 10.
Trap 3 · adding half an hour to the minutes 7:15 p.m. plus 2½ hours: add the 2 hours first (9:15), then the half hour (9:45). Doing it in one jump is where a 9:65 appears.
