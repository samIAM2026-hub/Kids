👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 118–120

✅ You did it 3 × 2 = 6, so 30 × 2 = 60 and 300 × 2 = 600. The same fact, just tens or hundreds.
The rule: multiply the ones first, then the tens, then the hundreds. Write each answer under its own column. The answer is called the product.
