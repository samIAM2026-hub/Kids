Kilograms and Grams

1 kg = 1000 g. To change kg into g, times the kg by 1000 and add the grams. To change g into kg and g, the thousands are the kg.

👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 29–30

✅ You did it1 kg = 1000 g. Read a scale in two steps: the big number the pointer has passed (kg), then the small markings after it (g).
kg → g: 1 kg 500 g = 1000 g + 500 g = 1500 g.
g → kg and g: 3450 g = 3000 g + 450 g = 3 kg 450 g.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book page 31

✅ All doneBefore you count small markings, find out how much each one is: count the steps from 0 to 1 and share 1000 g.
5805 g = 5000 g + 805 g = 5 kg 805 g — the last three digits are the grams.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finderMistakes to watch for: writing the kg and g numbers side by side (3 kg 6 g is 3006 g, not 36 g), using 1 kg = 100 g, reading every small marking as 100 g when it may be 50 g, and dropping a zero (805 g is not 85 g).
