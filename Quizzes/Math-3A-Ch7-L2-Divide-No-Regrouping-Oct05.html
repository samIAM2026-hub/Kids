✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: leaving out a 0 (303 ÷ 3 is 101, not 11), starting from the ones (642 ÷ 2 is 321, not 123), and a wrong fact (9 × 7 = 63, not 72). Check: answer × the number you divided by = the number you started with.
