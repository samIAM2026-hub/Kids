    🎚 发音设置 · Voice

    Devices ship several Chinese voices — some are much better than others. Pick one and test it.

    Best picks: in Chrome choose Google 普通话（中国大陆） — it is a network voice and sounds the most natural. On a Mac/iPad, look for Tingting / 婷婷 and prefer the Enhanced / Premium version; download it once in System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices → Chinese (China), then reload this page. Avoid anything labelled Compact, and avoid 粤语 / 台湾 voices — they read the story with the wrong accent.
    If none of them sounds right, use the read-aloud sheet on the menu and read the sentences yourself.
