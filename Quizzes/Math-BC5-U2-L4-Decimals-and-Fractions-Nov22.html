👀 Watch it✏️ Your turn🔍 Spot the mistake🏆 Test

A decimal is a fraction with 10, 100 or 1000 on the bottom. The place of the last digit tells you the bottom: tenths → 10, hundredths → 100, thousandths → 1000. The digits after the point are the top. 0.61 = 61/100.

Zeros at the end change nothing: 0.3 = 0.30 = 0.300.

✅ You did it Decimal → fraction: the place of the last digit is the bottom (10, 100, 1000); the digits after the point are the top.
Fraction → decimal: the bottom tells you how many places after the point (10 → 1, 100 → 2, 1000 → 3). Fill empty places with 0.
Equivalent decimals: add zeros to the right of the last digit — 0.4 = 0.40 = 0.400.
