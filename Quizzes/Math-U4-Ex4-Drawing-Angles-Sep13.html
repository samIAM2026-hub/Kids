✏️ Drawing an angle of a given size

Measuring runs backwards: instead of reading a number off, you mark one on and join it up.

1
Draw the base arm first with a ruler, and put a dot on it for the corner.
2
Centre the protractor on the dot, baseline along the arm.
3
Count from the 0 your arm is on up to the size you want, and make a small neat mark.
4
Join the dot to the mark with a ruler, and label the angle.

Check it afterwards by measuring what you drew. Drawing 55° and measuring 125° means you counted up the wrong scale — the same trap as Exercise 3, running the other way.

🔁 Angles bigger than 180°

A protractor only goes up to 180°, so an angle of 240° cannot be marked directly. Draw the other side of the turn instead.

360° − 240° = 120°  →  draw 120°, then mark the angle on the outside

An angle bigger than 180° is called a reflex angle. The two angles at the same corner always add up to 360°, so this subtraction is the whole technique.
