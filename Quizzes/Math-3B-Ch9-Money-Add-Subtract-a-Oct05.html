👀 1 · Watch it — Textbook pages 7–10

✅ You did itTwo ways to add money:
① Dollars, then cents: $3.20 + $1.55 → $3 + $1 = $4, 20¢ + 55¢ = 75¢ → $4.75.
② Make a whole dollar: $6.70 + 80¢ → $6.70 + 30¢ = $7, then + 50¢ → $7.50.
In columns: line up the dots and add like whole numbers.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — Textbook page 11

Guided Practice 1–3. Fill in the boxes line by line, or in columns.

✅ All doneCents only go up to 99. When they make 100¢ or more, change 100¢ into $1 and add it to the dollars.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finderThe traps: forgetting the $1 made from 100¢, writing 3 digits after the dot, and not lining up the dots.
