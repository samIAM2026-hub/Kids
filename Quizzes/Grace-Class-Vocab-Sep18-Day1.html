📖 The words 🎯 The test

Hi Grace! Your teacher wants all the class words learned this week, five a day. These are the teacher's own definitions — learn them the way they are written.

Read the word cards first (not timed). Then take the test: 10 questions, 80% to pass.

📖 The words

adj.
transient

Lasting only for a short time

Snowflakes on a warm window are transient — they melt in seconds.

adj.
makeshift

Making a temporary substitute

We used a cardboard box as a makeshift table for our picnic.

v.
preserve

To keep safe

Grandma keeps her old letters in a box to preserve them.

n.
notion

An idea, a belief

Where did you get the notion that snails can fly?

n.
attachment

Affection, fondness

Mia has a strong attachment to her grandma's old scarf.

🎯 The test — 80% to pass

10 questions — some ask what a word means, some ask which word fits a sentence. You need 8 correct to pass.
