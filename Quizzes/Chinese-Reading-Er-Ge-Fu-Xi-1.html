🎙️ Read it out loud — record it · 2 takes today

This is the last step. Read all three rhymes from the first line to the last — that is one take. Listen back, then do take 2. Each take is saved on its own the moment you stop, and your parents can listen to it on their page.

Before you press record
  • Say the number first: 一, 二, 三, then read that rhyme. Take a breath between rhymes.
  • Keep the beat. Rhyme 1 goes in fives: 一二三四五 · 金木水火土. Rhyme 3 goes in threes: 日月明 · 田力男.
  • 一 changes its tone: 一个 = yí gè, but 一头 · 一只 · 一群 · 一颗 · 一堆 = yì.
  • 堆 = duī, 独 = dú, 条 = tiáo — the three small pinyin printed on your sheet.
