⭐ The one rule: say the relationship BEFORE you look at the answers

Every analogy is really asking: what is the exact link between these two words? If you build that sentence first, four of the five choices usually eliminate themselves.

  • 1. Read the two words and make a short sentence that joins them.
  • 2. Cover the choices. Say your sentence out loud.
  • 3. Now try your sentence on each choice, in the same order.
  • 4. If two choices both work, make your sentence more specific and try again.
Example. "Puppy is to dog" → a puppy is the young of a dog. Now test: chicken–egg? No, backwards. Frog–snake? No link. Cygnet–swan? Yes — a cygnet is the young of a swan.
