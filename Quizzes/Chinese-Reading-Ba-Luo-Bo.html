🎙️ Read it out loud — record it · 2 takes today

This is the last step. Read the whole text from the first line to the last — that is one take. Listen back, then do take 2. Each take is saved on its own the moment you stop, and your parents can listen to it on their page.

Before you press record
  • Eight paragraphs, and they grow. Each helper adds one more link: 小狗拉着小姑娘，小姑娘拉着老婆婆… Pause a tiny bit at every ，so each link is clear.
  • “嗨哟！嗨哟！” — this is everyone pulling hard. Say it with lots of energy, like you are pulling too.
  • 拔呀拔—— the long dash means: hold it… wait a moment… and then the happy ending.
  • The last line is the point: 人多力量大，萝卜终于拔出来了！ Slow down and make it sound proud.
