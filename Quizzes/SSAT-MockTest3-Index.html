Middle Level SSAT · Summer Mock Test 3

One section at a time

The whole paper, split into six timed sittings. Each one is a real test: no answers while you work, then a score and a full explanation for every question.

Why it is split this way

The real SSAT runs about 1 minute per math question, 1 minute per reading question and 30 seconds per verbal question. Every sitting below keeps that pace, so the practice is honest — it is only the sitting length that is shorter.

Writing sample · do this one on paper
The essay
Haste makes waste.

Assignment: do you agree or disagree with the topic statement? Support your position with examples from your own experience, the experience of others, current events, history, or literature.

The writing sample is not scored — schools read it. Give it 25 minutes, handwritten, two pages. Two or three developed examples beat five thin ones.
