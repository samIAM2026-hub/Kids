👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 104–106

✅ You did it Dividing? Find the times-table fact. 18 ÷ 6 = ? → 3 × 6 = 18, so 18 ÷ 6 = 3.
One picture, two stories: 18 muffins on 6 trays → 3 on each tray. 18 muffins on trays of 3 → 6 trays.
Fact family: 3 × 8 = 24, 8 × 3 = 24, 24 ÷ 8 = 3, 24 ÷ 3 = 8.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 106–108

✅ All 5 done 48 ÷ 8 = ? Ask yourself: 8 × what = 48? 8 × 6 = 48, so 48 ÷ 8 = 6.
Not sure? Count by 8s until you reach 48: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48 — that is 6 eights.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: multiplying when you should divide (the answer gets bigger, not smaller), writing the number you divide by (35 ÷ 7 is not 7), taking away just once, and the fact next door (7 × 8 = 56, but 6 × 8 = 48). Always check with multiplication: answer × the number you divided by = the total.
