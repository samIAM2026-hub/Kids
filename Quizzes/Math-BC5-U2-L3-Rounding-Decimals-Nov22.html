Rounding Decimals

To round, find the place you are rounding to, then look at just ONE digit — the one to its right. 5 or more → round up. Less than 5 → keep it. Then drop every digit after that place.

Watch 4 examples, round 6 numbers yourself, check 5 answers for mistakes — then take the 20-question test (1 minute each · pass = 80%).
