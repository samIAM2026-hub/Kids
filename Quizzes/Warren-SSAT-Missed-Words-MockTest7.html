infuse v.

To fill something with a feeling or quality; to pour in so it spreads all through

On the SSAT it pairs with inject

The new coach infused the team with confidence.

💡 in- means in. Think of a tea bag: the flavour infuses into the hot water.

⚠️ release goes the opposite way — out, not in.

v.
fortify
tap to flip
fortify v.

To make stronger

On the SSAT it pairs with strengthen

The villagers fortified the walls with thick logs.

💡 A fort is a strong building. To fort-ify is to make something strong.

⚠️ weaken is the mirror-image trap.

v.
capitulate
tap to flip
capitulate v.

To give in; to surrender

On the SSAT it pairs with yield

After an hour of begging, Mom finally capitulated and let us stay up late.

💡 It sounds like capital, but it has nothing to do with cities or money. It is about giving up.

⚠️ resist is the opposite.

ramshackle adj.

Falling apart; badly built or badly looked after

On the SSAT it pairs with dilapidated

The ramshackle shed leaned to one side and had holes in the roof.

💡 Picture a ram crashing into a shed until everything is loose and broken.

⚠️ destructive means it wrecks other things. Ramshackle means it is the wrecked one.

adj.
open-minded

open-minded adj.

Willing to listen to and try new ideas

On the SSAT it pairs with flexible

Be open-minded about the new food — you might like it.

💡 A mind that is open lets new ideas in. It bends, like something flexible.

⚠️ wise is tempting, but being open to ideas is not the same thing as being wise.

adj.
eerie
tap to flip
eerie adj.

Strange in a quiet, spooky way that makes your skin prickle

On the SSAT it pairs with uncanny

The empty school at night was eerie.

💡 Say it slowly: ee-rie. It is the sound a creaky door makes in a quiet house.

⚠️ tranquil is quiet too, but calm and peaceful. Eerie is quiet and creepy.

adj.
homogenous
tap to flip
homogenous adj.

All the same all the way through

On the SSAT it pairs with uniform

Stir the paint until it is homogenous, with no streaks of another colour.

💡 homo- means same (like homonym, a word that sounds the same).

⚠️ varied is the opposite. unique means one of a kind — also the opposite.

v.
perforate
tap to flip
perforate v.

To make a hole or holes through something

On the SSAT it pairs with puncture

The machine perforates the paper so the tickets tear off neatly.

💡 per- means through (like permeate). Stamps and tickets have perforated edges.

⚠️ impale is to stick something onto a spike — not the same as making holes.

n.
disquiet

disquiet n.

A feeling of worry or unease

On the SSAT it pairs with unease

The strange creak on the stairs filled him with disquiet.

💡 dis- means not. Not quiet inside you — your mind will not settle.

⚠️ noise and loudness are traps: it is about feelings, not sound. composure (calm) is the opposite.

n.
facet
tap to flip
facet n.

One side or part of something

On the SSAT it pairs with aspect

Teamwork is one facet of being a good captain; courage is another.

💡 A cut diamond has many flat faces called facets. Each one is a single side.

⚠️ entirety means the whole thing — a facet is only one part.

v.
abate
tap to flip
abate v.

To become less strong; to die down

On the SSAT it pairs with diminish

We waited in the car until the heavy rain abated.

💡 You may know unabated — it means it never let up. So abate = let up, get weaker.

⚠️ Nothing about burning or power — just getting smaller.

adj.
taciturn
tap to flip
taciturn adj.

Saying very little; quiet by nature

On the SSAT it pairs with reticent

My taciturn grandpa answers most questions with one word.

💡 tacit means unspoken. A taciturn person leaves most things unspoken.

⚠️ humble is the trap: quiet people can seem modest, but taciturn is about talking little.

v.
redress
tap to flip
redress v.

To put a wrong right; to make up for it

On the SSAT it pairs with remedy

The shop redressed its mistake by giving me a full refund.

💡 Think of re-dressing a wound: you fix it and make it better.

⚠️ clothing is word-shape bait (re + dress). counteract means to work against, not to repair.

adj.
circumspect
tap to flip
circumspect adj.

Careful; looks at everything before acting

On the SSAT it pairs with cautious

A circumspect climber checks every rope twice.

💡 circum- = around (circumference) + -spect = look (inspect). Looking all around before you move.

⚠️ Do not mix it up with suspect. Circumspect is about being careful.

adj.
capricious
tap to flip
capricious adj.

Changing suddenly for no good reason

On the SSAT it pairs with fickle

The capricious weather went from sunshine to hail in ten minutes.

💡 A capricious person changes their mind like a young goat (capra) jumps about.

⚠️ sly adds sneaky cunning. Capricious just means unpredictable.
