👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 139–143

✅ You did it Long division, one place at a time: divide → multiply → subtract → bring down. Then do it again.
Left over? Regroup it: 1 hundred = 10 tens, 1 ten = 10 ones.
Too small to share (like 1 ten ÷ 3)? Write 0 in the quotient, then bring down.
Check: 3 × 134 + 2 = 404 ✓

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book page 144

✅ All 9 done Every step is the same four moves: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down.
The remainder must always be smaller than the number you divide by.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: a remainder that is too big (it must be smaller than the number you divide by), a missing 0 in the quotient, not regrouping what is left over, and stopping before the last digit. Always check: quotient × divisor + remainder = the number you started with.
