小松鼠找花生

“Little Squirrel Looks for the Peanuts” · xiǎo sōng shǔ zhǎo huā shēng

Read the whole story aloud twice · then play the pick-the-character game

What to do on this page: first read the story out loud — twice, once with the pinyin showing and once with it covered. Then play the pick-the-character game: every sentence of the story comes back with one character missing, and you click the right one. The pinyin is always given, so you are choosing between characters that sound the same — that is the hard part in Chinese, and the whole point of the game. 先把《小松鼠找花生》朗读两遍（第一遍看拼音，第二遍盖住拼音），再玩选字游戏——全文每句话都会出现一次。

Peanut flowers bend down and push into the soil, and the peanuts grow underground — 花生长在地下. He was looking in exactly the wrong place. Go and tell someone that.

🖱️ Pick the character

Five rounds, following the story from beginning to end — every sentence appears. One character is missing; the pinyin tells you how it sounds, and you click the character that writes that sound here. Get it on the first click and you win a ⭐. Get it wrong and the page tells you what the right one means and how to tell them apart — then you try again.
