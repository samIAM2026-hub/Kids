🐰 《小白兔和小灰兔》听句子 · 摆汉字

The whole story, chunk by chunk. Listen to one chunk — then put its characters in the right order.

How to play: Press 🔊 读一遍 and listen carefully. The words stay hidden.
Drag a character tile into the box where it belongs — or just tap it and it drops into the next empty box.
Tap a filled box to send that character back. Then press 检查 Check.
If something is wrong, the page tells you exactly which box is wrong. Stuck? 👀 偷看 shows the chunk for 4 seconds.
👨‍👩‍👧 给家长 · Read-aloud sheet (if you'd rather read the chunks yourself)
    Read each chunk once, at normal speed. Let the child hunt for the characters — don't point. If the built chunk is wrong, read it once more before checking.

    🎚 发音设置 · Voice

    Devices ship several Chinese voices — some are much better than others. Pick one and test it.

    Best picks: in Chrome choose Google 普通话（中国大陆） — it is a network voice and sounds the most natural. On a Mac/iPad, look for Tingting / 婷婷 and prefer the Enhanced / Premium version; download it once in System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices → Chinese (China), then reload this page. Avoid anything labelled Compact, and avoid 粤语 / 台湾 voices — they read the story with the wrong accent.
    If none of them sounds right, use the read-aloud sheet on the menu and read the chunks yourself.
