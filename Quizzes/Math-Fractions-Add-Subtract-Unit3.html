🔑 The one big idea

You can only add or subtract slices that are the same size.

Imagine a pizza cut into halves and another cut into quarters. If someone asks "what is one half take away one quarter?", you can't do it while the slices are different sizes. So you re-cut the halves into quarters — the amount of pizza doesn't change, only the number of cuts. Now both are quarters, and you can just count.

1
Same bottom
Make both denominators match. Usually you only need to re-cut one of them.
2
Add / take away the tops
The bottom number does not change. Only the top numbers move.
3
Simplify
Divide top and bottom by the same number until you can't any more.
