🧊 Face, edge, vertex

Three words, and they are easy to keep apart once you know where to look on the solid.

WordWhat it isOn a dice
Facea flat surfacethe side you read the number off
Edgethe line where two faces meeta straight side you could run a finger along
Vertexa corner where edges meeta point you could prick yourself on

Vertices is just the plural of vertex — more than one corner.

🔢 Counting without missing any

The hard part is the faces and edges you cannot see round the back. Count in groups instead of hunting over the picture:

1
Faces: top, bottom, then the ones round the side. A rectangular prism is 1 + 1 + 4 = 6.
2
Edges: round the top, round the bottom, then the uprights joining them. A rectangular prism is 4 + 4 + 4 = 12.
3
Vertices: the corners of the top face plus the corners of the bottom face: 4 + 4 = 8.

A free check. For every prism and pyramid, faces + vertices = edges + 2. The rectangular prism: 6 + 8 = 12 + 2 ✔. If your three numbers fail that, one of them is wrong.

Prism or pyramid? A prism has the same shape at both ends with rectangles joining them. A pyramid has one flat base and triangles rising to a single point at the top. The first word tells you the shape of the base.
