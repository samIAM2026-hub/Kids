🧭 Quick reminder before you start

The line in a fraction means divide. 74 means 7 ÷ 4: the top number is the thing being shared, the bottom number is how many shares.

1
Divide the top number by the bottom number.
2
The answer to the division is the whole number.
3
The remainder goes on top; the bottom number stays exactly the same.
4
Remainder 0? Then the answer is a plain whole number — no fraction part.
8/3 = 8 ÷ 3 = 2 remainder 2 → 2 and 2/3
Trap 1 — dividing the wrong way round83 is 8 ÷ 3, never 3 ÷ 8. An improper fraction is always more than 1, so an answer smaller than 1 means you divided backwards.
Trap 2 — putting the remainder on the bottom11 ÷ 4 = 2 remainder 3 gives 2 and 3/4, not 2 and 4/3. The bottom number never changes.
Trap 3 — a remainder bigger than the divisor"20 ÷ 3 = 5 remainder 5" is not finished: 5 is bigger than 3, so another 3 still fits → 6 remainder 2. The remainder must be smaller than the number you divided by.
