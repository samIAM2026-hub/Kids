✋ The hand family

This text is full of hands. Learn the radical once and a whole row of characters comes with it — that works far better than learning seventeen separate pinyin spellings.

✋ 扌 — the hand radical

Everything you do with your hands gets this radical on the left: 拉 pull · 摘 pick · 拾 gather up · 把 hold/grip. And 拿 (to take) has a whole 手 — a hand — sitting underneath it. See 扌 → think hand.
拾 vs 给
Look-alike shapes, different left side: 拾 is 扌 (hand) — you gather things up with hands. 给 is 纟 (silk thread) — giving a gift.
摘 vs 滴
摘 is 扌 (hand) — you pick vegetables with your hand. 滴 is 氵 (water) — a drop of water.
篮 vs 蓝
篮 has ⺮ (bamboo) on top, because baskets are woven from bamboo. 蓝 has 艹 (grass) — it is the colour blue.
跑
The left side is ⻊, a foot. Any character with a foot beside it is about going somewhere: 跑 run · 跳 jump · 路 road.

🌿 Doubled words · 4 from the text

Chinese loves saying a word twice. It makes things sound softer, livelier, or more complete. These four are all in the text.

Say each one out loud, then try to make your own: 红红的 (red-red), 高高的 (tall-tall), 认认真真 (really carefully). Can you find one more AABB word yourself?

🧺 The 把 sentence · 3 to rebuild

A 把 sentence tells you what happened to the thing. The pattern is always the same: who + 把 + the thing + what was done to it. From the text: 小朋友把小菜篮拿好。 · 大家把菜全部收完。 Tap the pieces in the right order.
