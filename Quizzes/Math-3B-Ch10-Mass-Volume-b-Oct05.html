👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 32–35

✅ You did it1 ℓ = 1000 ml. To read a measuring cup: how much is each marking? (top number ÷ 10), then count up to the top of the water.
Volume is how much liquid is in a container. Capacity is the most it can hold.
1 ℓ 500 ml = 1500 ml · 8725 ml = 8 ℓ 725 ml.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book page 34 and 37

✅ All doneDifferent cups have different markings: a 1 ℓ cup goes up in 100 ml, a 500 ml cup in 50 ml, a 100 ml cup in 10 ml.
When the water is in many cups, read each one, add them all, then change 1000 ml into 1 ℓ.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finderMistakes to watch for: writing the ℓ and ml numbers side by side (3 ℓ 7 ml is 3007 ml, not 37 ml), using 1 ℓ = 100 ml, reading every marking as 100 ml when it may be 50 ml or 10 ml, and dropping a zero (780 ml is not 78 ml).
