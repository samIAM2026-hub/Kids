🖼️ Big minus small — that is the whole exercise

Every figure in Exercise 6 is a rectangle with a smaller rectangle sitting inside it, and the shaded part is the frame left over.

shaded = big rectangle − small rectangle

There is no new formula here at all. The only work is finding the two pairs of sides — and that is where every lost mark lives.

➕ A border "2 m wide" makes the outside 4 m bigger

This is the one thing to get right. A border, path or frame runs down both sides, so a border w wide adds w twice to the width and w twice to the height.

Border widthAdds to the widthAdds to the height
1 m2 m2 m
2 m4 m4 m
3 cm6 cm6 cm

And it works the same going inwards. A towel 96 cm by 60 cm with a 3 cm border has an inside of 90 cm by 54 cm, not 93 by 57.

outside 96 × 60 = 5760 cm²  ·  inside 90 × 54 = 4860 cm² border = 5760 − 4860 = 900 cm²

🧭 Which rectangle were you given?

Read the question twice and decide whether the measurements you have describe the inside or the outside. Then go the right way.

The question saysYou were givenSo you
"a pool 20 m by 12 m, with a path 1 m wide round it"the insideadd 2 m to each side → 22 × 14
"a towel 96 cm by 60 cm with a 3 cm border"the outsidetake 6 cm off each side → 90 × 54

Getting this backwards is the single most common mistake in the whole of Unit 5, and the numbers still look sensible, so nothing warns you. The words around it do not settle it — the pool and the towel both say that. Ask yourself: is the border part of the thing I was given (a towel's border is part of the towel → go inwards), or is it added on outside it (a path round a pool → go outwards)?

⚠️ The three traps in this exercise

Trap 1 · adding the border once instead of twice A 1 m path round a 20 m by 12 m pool gives 22 m by 14 m, not 21 m by 13 m.
Trap 2 · adding up the four strips instead It is tempting to work out the four strips of the frame one at a time. It can be done, but the corners get counted twice. Big minus small is one subtraction and no corners.
Trap 3 · answering with the big rectangle The question asks for the shaded part — the frame. Finding 308 m² is the first half; the answer is 308 − 240 = 68 m².
