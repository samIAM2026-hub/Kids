🧭 Quick reminder before you start

In a set fraction the whole group is the 1. The bottom number is how many things (or equal groups) there are altogether; the top number is how many you are talking about.

1
Equal parts: total ÷ number of parts = how many in each part.
2
What fraction is shaded: shaded on top, everything (shaded and not) on the bottom.
3
Already in equal groups? Count groups instead of things.
4
Simplify — divide top and bottom by a number that goes into both.
6 flowers shaded out of 9 → 6/9 → 2/3
Trap 1 — how many lines?To make 4 parts you draw 3 lines. It is always one fewer line than parts, like slicing bread.
Trap 2 — shaded compared with unshaded6 shaded out of 9 is 69, not 63. The bottom number is the whole set, and that includes the shaded ones.
Trap 3 — forgetting a bunchIf the black beads are in two bunches, add both bunches together before you write the top number.
