🦡 Meet 獾 — the badger

The little animal in the story who is learning carpentry.

獾huān

A badger is a chunky, short-legged forest animal with strong claws — it is a champion digger. Badgers sleep in their burrows for most of the day and come out around dusk to look for food. They are easy to recognise: black-and-white stripes down the face.

Read the radical, guess the meaning:
獾 = 犭 (the "dog" radical) + 雚 (gives the sound huān).
Characters with 犭 are almost always animals: 猫 cat · 狗 dog · 狮 lion · 猴 monkey · 猪 pig · 狼 wolf. See 犭 → think animal.

In the story the badger is learning woodwork. First he makes three rough (粗糙) little stools. Hedgehog praises (称赞) him, that gives him confidence (自信), and soon he can make a whole chair.
