Main Idea, Best Title, and What the Passage Never Said

Reading A was 12 out of 22, and five of the ten misses were the same question in different clothes: best title, main purpose, what the passage implies. Every time, the choice you picked was true of one line of the passage rather than of the whole thing.

This page fixes one habit: say what the passage is doing before you read a single choice.

📋 1 · The routine

1
Read the first and last sentence of every paragraph first. A passage almost always announces its job in one of them.
2
Say the job in one sentence, in your own words, with the choices still covered. If you cannot, you are not ready to choose.
3
Now test each choice against that sentence and kill it for a named reason: too narrow (true of one line only) · wrong tone (far more serious or more excited than the passage) · not on the page (the passage never says it).
4
For a detail question, find the line before you pick. If the event in the question is not on the page, “the passage does not say” is the answer — not a trick.

Four passages from Mock Test 8. For each one: name the job, then answer the real question.
