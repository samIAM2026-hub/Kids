✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 68–74

✅ All 8 done To order three fractions, find the smallest and the greatest first. Comparing each one with 12 often sorts them fast.
Then read the question again: does it start with the greatest or the smallest?

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Traps to watch for: thinking a bigger bottom number means a bigger fraction (it means smaller parts), looking only at the top numbers when the parts are different sizes, and changing only the bottom number (multiply the top and the bottom by the same number).
