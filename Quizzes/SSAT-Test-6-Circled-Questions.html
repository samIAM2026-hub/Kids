How this page works. Every circled question is here as a fresh attempt. Pick an answer before you look at anything else — the walkthrough only unlocks after you commit. Getting it wrong on purpose teaches you nothing; getting it wrong honestly teaches you a lot.

Each solution has three parts: the steps (how to actually do it), the trap (why the tempting wrong answer is tempting), and the rule (the one sentence to carry into the next test). The rules matter more than the answers — there are only about a dozen of them in the whole SSAT.

Scoring reminder: +1 right, −¼ wrong, 0 blank. A blind five-way guess is worth zero on average — but cross out even one choice and guessing pays. So: eliminate, commit, move on.
