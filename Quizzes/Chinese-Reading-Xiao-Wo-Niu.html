Before you press record
  • Two voices: make 蜗牛妈妈 calm and kind, and 小蜗牛 small and excited.
  • 爬呀，爬呀 — read it slowly, like a snail crawling. Pause at each comma.
  • 哦，已经是夏天了！ — Mum is surprised. Say 哦 with a little rise, then a pause.
  • 碧绿碧绿的 = bì lǜ bì lǜ de. 待在家里 = dāi, not dài.
