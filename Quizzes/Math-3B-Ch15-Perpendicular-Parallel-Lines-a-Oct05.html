Perpendicular lines meet at a right angle — a square corner. On squared paper, count the squares each line goes.

👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 115–119

✅ You did itPerpendicular = the lines meet at a square corner (a right angle). We write AB ⊥ CB.
Straight across + straight up always make a square corner.
Slanted lines: count the squares. If the numbers swap places and one goes up while the other goes down (1 right 2 down · 2 right 1 up), the corner is square.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 116 and 120

✅ All 6 doneTo draw a perpendicular line on squared paper: across ↔ up and down, and for a slanted line swap the numbers and flip up / down.
In your book, check with a set-square or the corner of a folded paper.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finderMistakes to watch for: meeting is not enough (the corner must be square), slanted lines can be perpendicular, length does not matter, and count the squares carefully.
