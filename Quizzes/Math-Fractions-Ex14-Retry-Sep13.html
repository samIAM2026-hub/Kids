🧭 Quick reminder before you start

14 of 8 is written 84 because "of" means split into 4 groups (8 ÷ 4), and a fraction line is a divide sign. 23 of 6 is written 2 × 63: one group first, then times by the top.

1
Divide by the bottom number. Big number? Break it into friendly pieces: 96 ÷ 6 → 60 ÷ 6 = 10 and 36 ÷ 6 = 6 → 16.
2
Halving tricks: ÷ 4 is halving twice, ÷ 8 is halving three times, ÷ 10 takes a zero off.
3
Times by the top number.
4
Check: times back (16 × 6 = 96), and ask "should this be more or less than half?"
7/10 of 120 = 7 × 120/10 = 7 × 12 = 84
Trap 1 — leaving 84 as it isHere 84 is a division waiting to be done: 8 ÷ 4 = 2.
Trap 2 — dividing by the top numberFor 5/6 of 36, divide by the 6, not the 5: 36 ÷ 6 = 6, then 6 × 5 = 30.
Trap 3 — a careless divisionMost lost marks here are division slips like 96 ÷ 6 = 15. Write it out, then check by timesing back.
