Unknown Word Drill · Round 2

18 more words you have never seen · same five moves

Warren · harder · now with triples

Same rules as Round 1. Eighteen more words, every one of them checked against every SSAT paper in your folder — none of them has ever appeared, and none was in Round 1. You will not be told what any of them mean until after you answer.

  1. Twins. Read only the five choices. Any two that mean the same thing — cross off both. Needs nothing from the big word.
  2. Word parts. Any chunk you have met before?
  3. Where have I heard it? Say it inside a real sentence.
  4. Charge — only if you actually have a feeling. "No feeling" is a proper answer.
  5. Commit. Always. A blank is worth the same as a blind guess: nothing.
New in this round: triples. Sometimes three of the five choices all mean the same thing. All three cancel — because if any one of them were right, the other two would be right too. That drops you from 5 choices to 2, and a guess there is worth +0.38 of a point. Three of the words below hide a triple. Look for them.
