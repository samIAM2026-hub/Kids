🎙️ Read it out loud — record it · 2 takes today

This is the last step. Read the whole story, title first — that is one take. Listen back, then do take 2. Each take is saved on its own the moment you stop, and your parents can listen to it on their page.

Before you press record
  • Say the title first: 溪边的小石子, then take a breath.
  • 一 changes its tone: 一堆 · 一颗 · 一小会儿 · 一直 = yì, but 一句话 = yí jù huà.
  • 露出来 = lù chū lái, 相同 = xiāng tóng, 一小会儿 ends in a quick huìr.
  • 守着 = shǒu zhe — a light, quick zhe.
  • Read the end slowly and softly: 听溪水轻轻唱歌 — let your voice get quiet, like the stream.
