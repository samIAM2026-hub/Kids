🎯 The guessing rule

A wrong answer costs ¼ point and a blank costs nothing. With five choices, a blind guess breaks even on average, so it isn't worth it. Once you can cross out even one choice, guessing comes out ahead. So: cross out at least one choice, then guess. If you can't cross out anything, skip it and keep moving.

🧮 Maths · Sections 1 and 4

14 cards · each one shows the full working

🧰 Maths toolkit

  1. Circle the numbers, then check your copy. Before you bubble, look back once at the numbers you used.1.50 became 1.05 (Q13). 2½ hours became 1½ (Q14).
  2. Is my answer a sensible size? 130% of 70 must be a bit more than 70. 26.4 out of 220 must be under 100%.Q9, Q12.
  3. "Cannot be determined" is usually a trap. First look for hidden facts in the figure, like two equal sides.Q16.
  4. Stuck on letters? Put in numbers. You did this on the a/b question and were one step from the answer.Q25 (Section 1).

📖 Reading · Section 2

18 cards · the line that proves the answer is shown under each question

🧰 Reading toolkit

  1. Find the line first. Put your finger on the sentence that proves your answer. No line means it isn't the answer, even if it's true in real life.Q5 (sense of smell), Q24 (desegregation).
  2. NOT and EXCEPT questions: look for the choice the passage contradicts.Q19, Q23.
  3. Read little words and numbers exactly. "Close to 500" is not "over 500". 4,020 is exactly double 2,010.Q20, Q37.
  4. Keep moving. About one minute per question. The last passage had four easy points you never reached.Q36, Q38, Q39.

🔤 Verbal · Section 3

16 cards · 10 synonyms and 6 analogies

🧰 Verbal toolkit

  1. Break the word into parts. pre- (before) + monition (warning) = forewarning.Q16 premonition, Q2 fidelity, Q5 affirmative.
  2. A synonym fits the same slot in a sentence. "Glass" is an example of transparent, not a word that means it.Q11.
  3. Analogies: say a bridge sentence. "A beaker is a tool a chemist uses." Then test each choice with that sentence.Q58, Q59.
  4. Know the word type. If the first pair is a tool and a person, the answer must be a person too.Q58: construction is not a person.
