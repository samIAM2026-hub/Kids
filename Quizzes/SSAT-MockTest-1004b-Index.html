One section at a time

The whole paper, split into six timed sittings. Each one is a real test: no answers while you work, then a score and a full explanation for every question.

Why it is split this way

The real SSAT runs about 1 minute per math question, 1 minute per reading question and 30 seconds per verbal question. Every sitting below keeps that pace, so the practice is honest — it is only the sitting length that is shorter. The reading section breaks between Passage 4 and Passage 5, so no passage is ever split across two sittings. Do one a day, or two on a long afternoon; the scores add up to a whole paper either way.

About the answer key. The answers on these pages come from the printed key for EDinsight SSAT Middle Level Practice Test #2 (filed here by its upload date). The key sheet was scanned in SSAT Test#2.pdf, but the questions themselves are in SSAT Test#3.pdf; every page used here carries the “Practice #2” footer. Every maths question was also worked out independently, twice — 49 of the 50 agree. The key has one plain mistake, so there the page marks the right answer and says so under the question:
  • Quantitative 2, Q24 — the notched figure’s perimeter is 6.5 + 6.5 + 4 + 1.5 + 1 + 3.5 = 23 (D). The key’s 21 treats it as a plain rectangle and loses the notch walls.
Three answers deserve a second look; each says so under its explanation:
  • Reading A, Q15 — the coal picker’s mood in the last two lines: absorbed (key) or satisfied.
  • Analogies, Q41 — annihilate : dissolve :: create : imagine (key) is a loose fit; no choice matches perfectly.
  • Analogies, Q59 — range : mountain :: variety : color (key); freeway : lane is also defensible.
Everything else is left exactly as printed, slips included (“so whet their appetite”, “scrapes burns”). Working around a paper’s own slips is part of the practice.
