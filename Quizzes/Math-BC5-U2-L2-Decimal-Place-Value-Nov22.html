Decimal Place Value: Reading and Writing Decimals

BC Grade 5 Math · Unit 2 · 2.3 Decimals

👀 Watch it✏️ Your turn🔍 Spot the mistake🏆 Test

Every digit has a place, and the place tells you what the digit is worth. After the point come tenths, hundredths and thousandths — each place is 10 times smaller than the one before. In words, the point is read as and, and the last word names the place of the last digit.
