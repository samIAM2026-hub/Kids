Perimeter = the length all the way around a shape. Add every side once. Area = how many squares are inside.

👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 140–145

✅ You did it Perimeter = the length around. On a grid, count every little side on the edge (across + up and down).
Area = the number of squares inside (cm², m²).
Same area does not always mean same perimeter.
Square: 4 equal sides → side + side + side + side. Rectangle: long + short + long + short.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 144 and 146

✅ All 8 done Count squares for area, count the edge for perimeter — they are different numbers.
A shape made of smaller tiles: first find the length of each big side, then add them.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Traps to watch for: adding only the 2 sides with numbers, counting squares (area) when you need the perimeter, leaving out a side, and using a small tile's side for a big shape.
