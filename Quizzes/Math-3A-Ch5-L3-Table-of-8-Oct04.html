👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 92–93

A shirt has 8 buttons. How many buttons do 6 shirts have?

✅ You did it Count by 8s: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64, 72, 80.
Use a fact you know: 6 × 8 = 5 × 8 + 1 × 8 = 40 + 8 = 48.  8 × 8 = 10 × 8 − 2 × 8 = 80 − 16 = 64.
And 5 × 8 = 8 × 5 — swapping the numbers gives the same answer.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 94–95

✅ All 4 done Groups × how many in each group. 4 packs with 8 strawberries each → 4 × 8 = 32.
6 and 7 eights: start at 5 × 8 = 40 and add. 8 and 9 eights: start at 10 × 8 = 80 and take away.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: the fact next door (7 × 8 = 56 and 8 × 8 = 64 are only 8 apart!), adding instead of multiplying, the wrong times table (63 is 9 × 7, not an 8-fact), and adding when you should take away. To check a fact, build it from one you know: 5 × 8 = 40 or 10 × 8 = 80.
