👀 1 · Watch it — book page 38

A theatre sold 2302 tickets on Saturday and 2019 tickets on Sunday. How many tickets were sold altogether?

✅ You did it 2302 + 2019 = 4321. There were 4321 tickets sold altogether.
The rule: 11 ones is too many for one column → keep 1 one, and carry 1 ten over. Write that carried 1 small, on top of the tens.
