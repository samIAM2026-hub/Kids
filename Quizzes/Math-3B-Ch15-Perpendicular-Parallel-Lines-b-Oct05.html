Parallel, Horizontal and Vertical Lines

My Pals Are Here! Maths 3B · Chapter 15 · Lessons 2 and 3

👀 Watch it✏️ Your turn🔍 Spot the mistake🏆 Test

Parallel lines never meet and stay the same distance apart — same slant. Horizontal = straight across like the floor. Vertical = straight up and down like a hanging string.

👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 121–127

✅ You did itParallel = never meet, always the same distance apart. We write AB // CD and mark the lines with arrowheads.
Slanted lines: count the squares. The same steps (2 right, 4 down and 2 right, 4 down) mean the same slant → parallel.
Horizontal = straight across. Vertical = straight up and down. They are perpendicular to each other.
