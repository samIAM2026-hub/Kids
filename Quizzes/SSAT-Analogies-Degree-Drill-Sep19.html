Degree Analogies — Which Way Does the Arrow Point?

SSAT Middle Level · Verbal · built from Mock Test 8 questions 35, 47 and 55

🏹 The arrow🔁 The reversed trap🏆 Timed drill

Degree pairs are not about meaning. They are about direction. Funny and hilarious are the same idea — one is just bigger. The test always offers you the same relationship pointing the wrong way.

The move: draw the arrow and put the bigger word on the arrowhead. Then the flipped choice looks obviously wrong instead of vaguely right. (Method Lab card 8 · Playbook card 17 · Trap 9)

🏹 1 · Which way does the arrow point?

For each pair, say whether it runs mild → strong or strong → mild. Ten of them. Go fast — this is a five-second decision.

✅ Arrow check You can feel the direction now. On the real test, say it out loud in your head before you read a single choice: “small word, arrow, big word.”

🔁 2 · The reversed trap

Both choices below have the right relationship. Only one points the right way. This is the exact trap that cost you questions 35, 47 and 55.

✅ Trap beaten When two choices are both the right kind of pair, you are looking at a direction question — nothing else. Put the arrowhead on the bigger word in the stem, then do the same to each choice.

🏆 3 · Timed drill — 20 analogies

20 questions · 30 seconds each (10 minutes in all) · pass mark 80% (16 right).

Not all of them are degree pairs — some are other bridges. Name the bridge first, every time.

The clock pauses while you read an explanation, so a wrong answer costs you nothing but pride. Anything you leave unanswered when time runs out is marked wrong.
