👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 101–102

The cubes are in 5 rows. Each row has 6 cubes.

✅ You did it Read it by rows or by columns — same dots, same total. 5 × 6 = 6 × 5 = 30.  3 × 8 = 8 × 3 = 24.
Hard fact? Turn it around. You know 7 × 8 = 56, so 8 × 7 = 56 too.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book page 103

✅ All 4 done The missing number is the same on both lines. 5 × 6 = 30, so 6 × 5 = 30.
To find it, count by the number you see until you reach the total: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30 → 5 sixes.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: the fact next door (8 × 7 = 56, but 9 × 7 = 63), adding instead of multiplying, the wrong times table, and putting the total in the box. To check a fact, turn it around: 8 × 7 = 7 × 8.
