📖 The words

v.
reside

To live in

Many kinds of fish reside in this lake.

n.
residence

A place to live, home

The castle was once the residence of a king.

n.
domicile

Home, residence

A hermit crab carries its domicile on its back.

phrase
to and fro

Back and forth

The puppy ran to and fro across the yard.

adv.
conversely

On the other hand

Some people love summer; conversely, others prefer winter.
