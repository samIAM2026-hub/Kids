👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 88–89

There are 4 groups of pupils. There are 7 pupils in each group. How many pupils are there?

✅ You did it Count by 7s: 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, 56, 63, 70.
Use a fact you know: 6 × 7 = 5 × 7 + 1 × 7 = 35 + 7 = 42.  8 × 7 = 10 × 7 − 2 × 7 = 70 − 14 = 56.
5 × 7 = 7 × 5 — swapping the numbers gives the same answer. And 0 × 7 = 0.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 90–91

✅ All 4 done Groups × how many in each group. 4 boxes with 7 pencils each → 4 × 7 = 28.
6 and 7 sevens: start at 5 × 7 = 35 and add. 8 and 9 sevens: start at 10 × 7 = 70 and take away.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: the fact next door (7 × 7 = 49 and 8 × 7 = 56 are only 7 apart!), adding instead of multiplying, the wrong times table, and adding when you should take away. To check a fact, build it from one you know: 5 × 7 = 35 or 10 × 7 = 70.
