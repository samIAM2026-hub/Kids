👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 122–127

✅ You did it 13 × 4 = 52 · 125 × 2 = 250 · 223 × 5 = 1115
The rule: multiply one column at a time, starting at the ones. If you get 10 or more, keep the ones digit and carry the rest to the next column. In the next column, multiply first, then add what you carried.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 124 and 128

✅ All 8 done Ones first. Multiply, then add the small carried number. If a column makes 10 or more, write the ones digit and carry the rest. A 0 times anything is 0 — but still add the carry!
