👀 1 · Watch it — Textbook pages 12–14

✅ You did itWays to take away money:
① Dollars, then cents: $42.75 − $20.30 → $22 and 45¢ → $22.45.
② Use a $1: $4.70 − 80¢ → $3.70 + (100¢ − 80¢) → $3.90.
③ Take away a round amount, then add back: $9.70 − $5 = $4.70, + 10¢ → $4.80.
In columns: line up the dots and regroup like whole numbers.
