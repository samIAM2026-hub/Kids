When things don't share out equally, the extra is the remainder: 11 ÷ 5 = 2 R 1. The remainder is always smaller than the number you divide by.

👀 1 · Watch it — book page 132

✅ You did it Share, then see what is left. 11 ÷ 5 = 2 R 1: 2 in each group, 1 left over.
Quotient = how many in each group (on top). Remainder = what is left (at the bottom).
The remainder must be smaller than the number you divide by. If it isn't, one more fits!

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book page 134

✅ Both done Three steps every time: how many fit (quotient) → multiply → subtract (remainder).
50 ÷ 6: 6 × 8 = 48, 50 − 48 = 2, so 50 ÷ 6 = 8 R 2.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: a remainder that is too big (another group still fits), forgetting the remainder, writing what you used instead of what is left, and a quotient that is too big (you need more than you have). Always check: divisor × quotient + remainder = the number, and the remainder is smaller than the divisor.
