Every question has five choices (A–E). Inside a section you can go back and forth; once time is up you can’t return to it.

2 · The guessing rule (−¼)

  1. Right = +1. Wrong = −¼. Blank = 0. Four wrong answers wipe out one right answer.
  2. Cross out at least ONE choice → guess. Can’t cross out any → leave it blank. A blind guess among 5 wins nothing on average. Once one choice is gone, every guess is worth a little more than zero — and two gone is even better.
  3. Never leave a question blank just because you are unsure. “I think it’s B or D” is a guess you must take. On Paper Test 10.9 you left 17 blank — many of those had a choice you could have crossed out.

3 · How to move through a section — two passes

  1. Pass 1: answer everything you can do in under a minute. Hard one? Cross out what you can, circle the number in the booklet, and move on. Don’t let one question eat three minutes.
  2. Pass 2: come back to the circled ones. Now spend the leftover time. Guess on any of them where you crossed out at least one choice.
  3. Check the clock at the halfway question. Math: question 13 by minute 15. Reading: passage 4 by minute 20. Verbal: done with synonyms by minute 12.
  4. Bubble carefully. When you skip a question, skip its row on the answer sheet too. Every 5 questions, check the number in the booklet matches the row you are bubbling.
  5. Write all over the booklet. Cross out wrong choices, write the math, underline key words. Only the answer sheet is marked.

4 · The writing sample

  1. Pick fast (2 minutes) and plan (3 minutes). Choose the prompt you can see a story for. Jot: who, what problem, how it ends.
  2. Finish the story. A short story with a real ending beats a long one that stops in the middle. Leave 3 minutes to read it over.
  3. Show, don’t tell — one or two strong details, a line of dialogue, a feeling.

5 · Test-day checklist

  • Sleep early the night before. No new practice tests tonight.
  • Breakfast. A water bottle and a snack for the break.
  • Admission ticket, ID if asked, sharp #2 pencils and an eraser. A watch without an alarm (no smartwatch).
  • No calculator, no rulers, no phone.
  • Arrive early. Take three slow breaths before each section.
  • One hard question is just one question. Circle it, move on, and don’t let it follow you into the next one.
