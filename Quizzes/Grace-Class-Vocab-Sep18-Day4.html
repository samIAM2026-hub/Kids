📖 The words

v.
empathize

To understand the feelings of others

I empathize with you — I was nervous on my first day too.

n.
empathy

The ability to understand the feelings of others

Showing empathy means imagining how someone else feels.

v.
acquiesce

To agree to something even though you don’t want to

The twins begged for pizza, and Dad finally acquiesced.

n.
acquiescence

Accepting something with hesitation

He nodded in quiet acquiescence, though he wished he could say no.

v.
concede

To admit, to accept

The team conceded that the other side had played better.

n.
concession

A thing that is granted

The store made a concession and let us return the toy without a receipt.

Word families todayempathize → empathy
acquiesce → acquiescence
concede → concession
