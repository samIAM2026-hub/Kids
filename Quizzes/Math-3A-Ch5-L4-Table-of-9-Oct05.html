👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 96–98

Lina bought 6 cupcakes. There are 9 raisins on each cupcake. How many raisins altogether?

✅ You did it Count by 9s: 9, 18, 27, 36, 45, 54, 63, 72, 81, 90.
Use a fact you know: 6 × 9 = 5 × 9 + 1 × 9 = 45 + 9 = 54.  8 × 9 = 10 × 9 − 2 × 9 = 90 − 18 = 72.
Finger trick: fold down finger 7 → 6 fingers before, 3 after → 7 × 9 = 63.
Check it: the two digits of a 9-fact always add up to 9 (6 + 3 = 9). And 5 × 9 = 9 × 5.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 99–100

✅ All 4 done Groups × how many in each group. 3 loaves with 9 slices each → 3 × 9 = 27.
6 and 7 nines: start at 5 × 9 = 45 and add. 8 and 9 nines: start at 10 × 9 = 90 and take away.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: the fact next door (6 × 9 = 54 and 7 × 9 = 63 are only 9 apart!), adding instead of multiplying, adding when you should take away (9 × 9 = 90 − 9, not 90 + 9), tens and ones swapped (72, not 27), and the wrong times table. Quick check: the digits of a 9-fact add up to 9.
