✅ Before your next mock — 5 habits

  1. Synonyms: if a choice sounds like another word hidden in the question word (entry in sentry, surprise in surmise), slow down — it is usually a trap.
  2. Analogies: make a sentence first. Then ask “same or opposite?” and “which way?” Only then look at the choices.
  3. Maths: before you click, read the last line of the question again. Is your number the thing it asks for?
  4. Reading: name the question type first (main idea / next / linking word / job / EXCEPT) and use its method.
  5. Guessing: cross off at least one choice before you guess. If you can’t, leave it blank (−¼ for wrong, 0 for blank).
