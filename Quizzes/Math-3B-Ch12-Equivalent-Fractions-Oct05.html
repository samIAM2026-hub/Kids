👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 56–60

✅ You did it Equivalent fractions are the same amount, written in a different way: 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6.
The rule: multiply the top and the bottom by the same number — or divide both by the same number. When no number (except 1) divides both, the fraction is in its simplest form.

✅ All 8 done First look at the number you know: how did it change (× 2? × 3? ÷ 4?). Then do the same thing to the other number.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Watch out for these: adding instead of multiplying, changing only the top or only the bottom, and stopping before the simplest form. Whatever you do to the top, do to the bottom!
