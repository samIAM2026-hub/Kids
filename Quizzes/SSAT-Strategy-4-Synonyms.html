Synonyms: predict, then pick

The first 30 questions of Verbal — about 12 minutes. Then try 14 words you missed before.

Your numbers

First tries on 9 synonym sittings: 60% right in about 12 minutes — on pace. On paper 10.9 you got 27/30, your best ever. In Mock 9.25c, 8 of your 12 misses took under 10 seconds. In September, 6 of 10 misses were a word that goes with the capital word, not one that means the same.

Your traps: too fast on half-known words, look-alike / sound-alike, and “goes with” instead of “means the same”.

1 · The routine (about 20 seconds)

  1. Cover the choices. Say your own meaning first. If you read A–E first, the traps choose for you. If your word is there, take it.
  2. Don’t know it? Find a phrase you’ve heard. “expedited shipping” → fast; “business acumen” → sharpness; “a speech impediment” → something in the way.
  3. Break it into parts. re + cede = go back (RECEDE → retreat). pre + monition = warning before. ten = hold (TENACIOUS). fid = faith (FIDELITY). Parts are a clue, not a proof.
  4. Do the swap test. Your pick must fit in the capital word’s place in a sentence. “Walked through the orchard” → “grove” works, “fruit” doesn’t.
  5. Then commit and move. Crossed out at least one? Guess. Nothing at all? Leave it blank and keep moving.

2 · Traps to cross out

Look-alike / sound-alike — DOGMATIC ≠ canine (dog), SENTRY ≠ beginning (entry), SURMISE ≠ surprise. Read every letter.
Goes with, not the same — TRANSPARENT → glass is an example; ORCHARD → fruit is connected. Neither means the same.
The opposite — the test almost always puts one in. TENACIOUS → timid. Spot it and cross it out.
Wrong word type — a describing word needs a describing word; an action needs an action.
Too strong / too weak — DETER → hinder, not halt. Check the strength.
Second meaning — if your meaning matches nothing, try the word’s other meaning (BLUNT = direct in speech, VESSEL = container).

3 · Free eliminations

  1. Twins cancel out. If two choices mean the same thing, neither can be the one answer.
  2. Word endings tell the type. -ous, -ful, -ive, -able describe; -tion, -ity, -ness, -ment are things; -ate, -ify, -ize are actions.
  3. Charge (+ / −) is the last tiebreaker, never your first move.
