You don't have to count every square. Area = Length × Breadth — and the answer is in cm² or m².

👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 147–148

✅ You did it Area = Length × Breadth. It is the same as counting the squares: rows × squares in each row.
Small things → cm². Big things like a rug → m².
6 × 3 and 3 × 6 are both 18 — you can multiply in either order.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 148–149

✅ All 5 done On squared paper, count along the top for the length and down the side for the breadth, then multiply.
A square has all sides the same, so its area is side × side (5 × 5 = 25).

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: adding the sides instead of multiplying, finding the perimeter (all the way round) instead of the area, using the wrong length for a side, and writing cm when it should be cm².
