👀 1 · Watch it — book pages 132–137

✅ You did it Area is how much space a shape covers. Count the squares.
Whole square = 1. Half square = ½. 2 half squares = 1 whole square.
A 1-cm square has an area of 1 cm² (1 square centimetre). A 1-m square has an area of 1 m² (1 square metre).

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 135, 138–139

✅ All 8 done Shapes can look different and still have the same area.
For a full rectangle of squares, count one row, then count the rows: 4 rows with 4 in each row = 16 squares.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: counting a half square as a whole one, forgetting the half squares, adding the sides instead of counting the squares, and counting one square twice.
