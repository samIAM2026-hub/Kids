Hi Grace! A mixed number is a whole number with a fraction stuck on the end — like 212, said "two and a half".

Win four badges, then take the test — 8 out of 10 to pass.

🍕 Game 1 · Pizza Shop

A mixed number is really just two things added together: some whole pizzas, plus a few extra slices.

Customers order in mixed numbers. Tap the round tray to add a whole pizza (tap a pizza to take it back), and tap the slices on the cut pizza.

Order 1 of 5 ☆☆☆☆☆
🏆 Badge earned · Pizza Shop Every order was whole pizzas + leftover slices. That plus sign is hiding inside every mixed number, even though nobody writes it.

✂️ Game 2 · Slice Shrinker

Same amount of pizza, bigger slices. That is all "simplest form" means: you group the little slices together until they will not group any more.

Pick a number to group by. It has to go into both the top and the bottom — and the whole number stays locked, it never changes.

Round 1 of 5 ☆☆☆☆☆
Group until nothing goes into both numbers, then press It's smallest.
🏆 Badge earned · Slice Shrinker Notice what never changed in any round — the whole number. Grouping only ever happens to the fraction on the right.

🐸 Game 3 · Frog Pond

The lily pads 🪷 are the whole numbers. Mixed numbers live in the water between them.

A fly 🪰 is hiding at the number on the card — it only comes out when the frog lands right on it. Tap anywhere on the pond to hop there, or use the buttons. Landing on a lily pad first is the fast way.

Round 1 of 5 ☆☆☆☆☆
🏆 Badge earned · Frog Pond Same two moves every time: land on the whole number, then count the small hops on from there. Never count the small hops from 0 — that is the mistake this lesson is really testing.

📘 Game 4 · The book's own questions

These five are straight out of page 9 — and you do them here, not on paper. Each one works like the game it came from. Stuck? Every question has a Show me how button.

🏆 Badge earned · Book Questions All five of page 9, done without a pencil. You are ready for the test.
Watch out 1 138 means 1 plus 38. Say "and" out loud and you will never read it as a times sum.
Watch out 2 3810 is right but not finished. Group it down to 345 — and leave the 3 alone.
Watch out 3 For 125, count the 2 small hops from the 1. Counting from 0 lands you a whole number too early.

🎯 The test — 80% to pass

10 questions, all from pages 7 to 9. You need 8 correct to pass.

⏱️ The clock is on for this part: 10 minutes, one minute a question. It starts when you press the button below and it does not stop. If a question sticks, put down your best answer and move on — a blank counts as wrong, a guess might not.

Keep paper and a pencil next to you. The games above are not timed, so play them first.
