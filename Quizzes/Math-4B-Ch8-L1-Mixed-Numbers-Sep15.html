Hi Grace! A mixed number is a whole number with a fraction stuck on the end — like 212, said "two and a half".

There are three games below. Each one teaches one thing, and each takes about a minute. Play all three, then take the test — 8 out of 10 to pass.

🎮 Game 1 · Build it

A mixed number is really just two things added together: some whole circles, plus a few extra slices.

Your job: make the picture match the number. Use the buttons.

Round 1 of 5 ☆☆☆☆☆
whole circles slices
🏆 Game 1 finished You just built five mixed numbers. Every single one was whole circles + leftover slices. That plus sign is hiding inside every mixed number, even though nobody writes it.

✂️ Game 2 · Cut it down

The fraction part has to be as small as it will go. So you keep dividing the top and the bottom by the same number until nothing goes into both.

Two rules for this game: the number you pick must go into both the top and the bottom — and the whole number never moves. It just sits there and watches.

Round 1 of 5 ☆☆☆☆☆
Keep cutting until nothing goes into both numbers, then press It's smallest.
🏆 Game 2 finished Notice what never changed in any round — the whole number. Cutting only ever happens to the fraction on the right.

🐸 Game 3 · Frog hops

On a number line the tall marks are the whole numbers. Mixed numbers live in the gaps between them.

Hop the frog onto the target. Big hops move a whole number; small hops move one little step. Big hops first is the fast way.

Round 1 of 5 ☆☆☆☆☆
🏆 Game 3 finished You did the same two moves every time: land on the whole number, then count the small steps on from there. Never count the small steps from 0 — that is the mistake this lesson is really testing.

✏️ Now the book's own questions

These five are straight out of page 9 — the same three things you just played. Try each one on paper first, then look.

Watch out 1 138 means 1 plus 38. Say "and" out loud and you will never read it as a times sum.
Watch out 2 3810 is right but not finished. Cut it down to 345 — and leave the 3 alone.
Watch out 3 For 125, count the 2 small steps from the 1. Counting from 0 lands you a whole number too early.

🎯 The test — 80% to pass

10 questions, all from pages 7 to 9. You need 8 correct to pass.

⏱️ The clock is on for this part: 10 minutes, one minute a question. It starts when you press the button below and it does not stop. If a question sticks, put down your best answer and move on — a blank counts as wrong, a guess might not.

Keep paper and a pencil next to you. The games above are not timed, so play them first.
