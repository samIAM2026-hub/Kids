🎙️ Read it out loud — record it · 2 takes today

This is the last step. Read the whole story, title first — that is one take. Listen back, then do take 2. Each take is saved on its own the moment you stop, and your parents can listen to it on their page.

Before you press record
  • Say the title first: 露珠 (lù zhū).
  • 一 changes its tone: 一颗颗 = yì kē kē, but 一看 = yí kàn and 一下 = yí xià.
  • 不 changes too: 不见了 = bú jiàn le.
  • 还会再来 = hái huì zài lái (it will come again) — not huán.
  • Read 风轻轻吹 and 风悄悄说 softly, like a quiet morning breeze. 小草 asks a question, so let your voice go up: 露珠去哪里了？
