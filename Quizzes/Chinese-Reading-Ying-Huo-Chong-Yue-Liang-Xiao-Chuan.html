Before you press record
  • Say the title first: 萤火虫 … then 月亮的小船. Take a breath between the two stories.
  • 着 has two sounds: 着急 = zháo jí and 睡着 = shuì zháo, but 提着 · 笑着 · 抬着 · 陪着 · 照着 = zhe.
  • 一 changes its tone: 一阵 = yí zhèn, but 一只 · 一直 · 一起 = yì.
  • 弟弟 = dì di, 摇摇头 = yáo yao tóu, 漂 and 飘 are both piāo.
  • Read 《月亮的小船》 softly and slowly — the moon is quiet (安安静静), so your voice can be too.
