2. Go from the end of the bar straight to the scale and read the number.
How many more / fewer? Take away.  Altogether? Add.  Twice as many? × 2.

✏️ 2 · Your turn — book pages 49–50

✅ All 8 done No bar means 0. Bars that end at the same place show the same number.
"Which month had 225 more than September?" → work out the number first (350 + 225 = 575), then find the bar that ends there.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder Mistakes to watch for: a small step is not always 1, adding when the question says "how many more", reading the wrong bar, and leaving out a bar when you add them all up.

🏆 4 · The test — Workbook pages 59–64

29 questions — all of Workbook Practice 1, pages 59–64 · 1 minute each (29 minutes in all) · pass mark 80% (24 right).

Type the number, or tap the name. Some questions need two names — tap both. Then press Check. You get one try per question — if it's wrong, you'll see why straight away.

Question 4(f) asks you to write a sentence. Here you just fill in the number. In your book, still write the sentence.
