🧭 First decide WHICH kind of question it is

Exercise 19 mixes everything from Unit 3 together, and that is the whole difficulty — you no longer get told which method to use. Before you calculate anything, read the question once and put it in one of these three boxes:

🕵️ The number the question makes you work out yourself

Several of these questions hide one of the two amounts. You have to build it before you can make the fraction:

1
Sara's curtains: the material used is not given — 6 curtains × 2 m = 12 m. Then 12 out of 40.
2
Travis's mangoes: the number sold is not given — $240 ÷ $2 = 120 mangoes. Then 160 − 120 = 40 left.
3
Stewart's money: the money left is not given — $25 − $5 = $20. Then 20 out of 25.

A good habit: write the two amounts on your paper as a pair, like 12 m out of 40 m, before you write any fraction at all.

⚠️ The three traps in this exercise

Trap 1 · using the wrong number as the whole For Sara the whole is the 40 m she bought, not the 6 curtains and not the 2 m. The whole is always the amount she started with.
Trap 2 · treating "15 ft left" as "15 ft used" Mary used 58, so the 15 ft is the other 3 boxes. 1 unit = 15 ÷ 3 = 5 ft, and the whole roll is 8 × 5 = 40 ft. Shade the bar first, every time.
Trap 3 · forgetting to simplify 40160 is correct but it is not finished — 14 is the answer the book wants.
