✅ All 9 done Work right to left: ones, tens, hundreds, thousands. Any column can regroup from the one on its left. Zeros in the way? Walk left until you find a digit that isn't 0. Every 0 you pass becomes 9. So 3000 = 2 thousands 9 hundreds 9 tens 10 ones.

🔍 3 · Spot the mistake

✅ Mistake finder The two big mistakes: flipping a column (bottom − top) instead of regrouping, and forgetting that the column that gave 1 away now has 1 less — a 0 that passed 1 along is 9, not 10. To check a subtraction, add: answer + bottom number = top number.
