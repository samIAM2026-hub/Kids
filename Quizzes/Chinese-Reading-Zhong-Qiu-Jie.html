🎙️ Read it out loud — record it · 2 takes today

This is the last step. Read the whole text from the first line to the last — that is one take. Listen back, then do take 2. Each take is saved on its own the moment you stop, and your parents can listen to it on their page.

Before you press record
  • Four paragraphs. Take a small breath at the end of each one before you start the next.
  • Stop at 。and pause at ， — breathe at the punctuation, not wherever you run out of air.
  • 又大又圆，闪闪亮亮 — this is the picture of the moon. Say it slowly and brightly, like you can see it.
  • The last line is the point: 团团圆圆、幸福美满. Slow down and make it sound happy.
