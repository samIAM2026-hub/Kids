👀 1 · Watch it — book page 50

Find the difference between 5987 and 864.  5987 − 864 = ?

✅ You did it 5987 − 864 = 5123. The difference between 5987 and 864 is 5123.
The rules: bigger number on top, ones under ones. Start at the ones and take the bottom digit away from the top digit. If nothing is underneath, the digit comes straight down.
A 0 in the middle must still be written: 2432 − 1331 = 1101 (book page 51).
